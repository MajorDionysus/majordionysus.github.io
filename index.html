About Me

I am Renjie (Rayn Jay) Mei, a enthusiastic undergraduate researcher specializing in Bio-Computer Interface and Flexible Electrode.

Currently, I am pursuing my ndergraduate degree in Intelligent Medical Engineering at The Southern University of Science and Technology, China. My research experiences center on metal-polymer electrodes for biomedical applications. I am particularly interested in developing innovative solutions for wearable health technologies and bio-computer interfaces.

Outside of academia, I enjoy ping-pong, billiards or snooker, photography, and exploring the intersection of technology and art. I believe that creativity and curiosity are the driving forces behind meaningful innovation.

BME YES! BUT WHY?

With human lifespan steadily increasing, the challenges associated with neurodegenerative diseases and chronic conditions are becoming more pressing. It is no longer sufficient for humans to live longer—we must also ensure a high quality of life, maintaining both physical and cognitive well-being. This calls for advanced biomedical technologies that integrate seamlessly with the human body, enabling early diagnosis, real-time monitoring, and effective intervention.

I'm thrilled to delve into research with a particular focus on the intersection of Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI) and flexible electrodes, aiming to develop next-generation neurotechnology solutions that enhance human health and performance. Currently, as an undergraduate student in Intelligent Medical Engineering at the Southern University of Science and Technology, I am exploring metal-polymer electrodes for biomedical applications and signal processing techniques to improve medical device functionality. My goal is to bridge the gap between engineering and medicine, contributing to the development of wearable health technologies and neural interfaces that offer both comfort and precision.

By leveraging interdisciplinary innovations in materials science, bioelectronics, and artificial intelligence, I aspire to create practical solutions that empower individuals to lead healthier, more fulfilling lives in an era of extended longevity.

Hometown

My hometown, DF, is a small town nestled at the foot of the majestic Songshan Mountain, renowned as one of China's Five Great Mountains. This region is steeped in history and culture, most famously as the home of the legendary Shaolin Temple, a cradle of Chan Buddhism and martial arts. Dengfeng offers a harmonious blend of ancient heritage and natural beauty, with scenic landscapes, tranquil temples, and vibrant local traditions. The area's unique atmosphere inspires both reflection and exploration, making it a truly special place to grow up. Grand Prix

The 2025 Grand Prix Final was an unforgettable experience for me as a fan and traveler. Held at the Hong Kong Kai Tak Sports Park, the event brought together world-class players and an enthusiastic crowd, creating an electric atmosphere. Watching top professionals, Robertson and Bingham, compete at such a high level was both thrilling and inspiring. Even more astonishing was Robertson's incredible 10-0 sweep -- a truly historic scoreline in snooker. Bingham, unfortunately, was far from his best. After the match, he revealed that he had been deeply affected by the recent passing of his uncle, which understandably impacted his performance.
